THE INFANTCRY PROCESS

From audio to a likely reason.

InfantCry takes a WAV recording through an audio-analysis pipeline and displays the predicted cry category returned by the model.

01

Provide audio

Record a cry through the microphone or select a WAV file from your device.

02

Analyze patterns

The audio-processing pipeline prepares the recording for the cry classifier.

03

View the reason

The result screen displays the predicted cry category returned by the API.

ⓘ The predicted category is an exploratory audio result, not a medical diagnosis.
