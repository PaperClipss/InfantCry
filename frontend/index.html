A SMALL PREPARATION

A clearer recording starts with a calmer moment.

  • 01 Choose a quieter space when possible.
  • 02 Keep the device at a safe distance from your baby.
  • 03 Capture the sound without delaying comfort or care.

Never leave a baby unattended to make a recording.

✦
Use the result as one small piece of context.

A model prediction is not a medical assessment. Check your baby's surroundings and needs, trust your observations, and contact a healthcare professional when you are concerned.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

A prediction is not an explanation of your baby's health.

InfantCry cannot diagnose illness, pain, hunger, discomfort, or an emergency. Different needs can sound similar. Check your baby's cues and surroundings, and contact a qualified healthcare professional whenever you are worried. Do not delay urgent care to record or analyze a cry.
