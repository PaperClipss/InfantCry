GET TO KNOW LITTLE VOICES

Understanding every cry.

Different needs can sound similar. Listen carefully and consider the whole situation.

♡

Could be hunger

Look for feeding cues, such as rooting, lip-smacking, or bringing hands to the mouth.

☁

Could be discomfort

Check the diaper, clothing, temperature, and whether your baby needs burping.

☾

Could be tiredness

Yawning, looking away, or fussing may mean your baby needs a calmer environment.

ⓘ These are general possibilities, not conclusions from the audio model. A cry alone cannot reliably identify a baby's needs.

GENTLE GUIDANCE

Care tips

Simple things to check while comforting your baby.

♡

Offer comfort

Try holding your baby close, speaking softly, or gently rocking them.

◷

Check the basics

Consider feeding, a nappy change, temperature, tiredness, or the need for a cuddle.

♫

Keep things calm

Reduce loud sounds and bright lights, and notice what helps your baby settle.

＋

Seek help when needed

If you are worried about your baby's health or crying, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Audio predictions are exploratory and are not a medical diagnosis.
